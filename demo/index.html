Paying for it

Most families start at $0 down.

Own it with a solar loan, or go solar-as-a-service with a lease or PPA — third-party-owned systems still capture federal clean-energy incentives in 2026 and pass the savings through to your rate.

$0-down solar loan

Own the system, fixed monthly payment — often at or below what you currently pay the utility. Florida charges no sales tax on residential solar, and the added home value is exempt from property tax.

Lease / PPA

No upfront cost, no maintenance responsibility — you simply buy the power at a locked, lower rate. The route most Tampa Bay families choose in 2026.

Cash purchase

Best lifetime return and shortest payback. We'll show you the honest math side-by-side with the loan and lease options — no steering.

Straight answers

Questions every homeowner asks.

What does solar cost in Florida in 2026?

Typical Tampa Bay homes run roughly $18,000–$28,000 before financing — about 10% below the national average — but the honest answer depends on your usage and roof. That's what the free audit is for. Florida waives sales tax on the system and won't raise your property taxes for the added value.

Is the 30% federal tax credit still available?

Not for cash or loan purchases — that credit ended December 31, 2025. Lease and PPA systems still capture federal clean-energy incentives through the system owner, which is passed through as a lower rate. Anyone telling you otherwise in 2026 is reading an old script — and that should tell you something about them.

Will panels survive a hurricane?

Our mounting systems are engineered and permitted to Florida's high-velocity wind zone standards (160+ mph rated hardware) and every attachment is inspected. Panels routinely survive storms that take down the power lines around them — which is exactly when the battery earns its keep.

What happens to my homeowner's insurance?

Rooftop solar is usually covered under your existing dwelling coverage; some carriers ask for a rider or an updated replacement value. We provide the documentation your insurer needs and it's typically a phone call, not a premium shock.

How does net metering work with my utility?

Extra power your panels produce flows to the grid and your utility credits you at the full retail rate, offsetting what you draw at night. Signing your interconnection agreement now locks that arrangement for 20 years even if rules change later.

How long does the whole process take?

Installation itself is 1–2 days. Start to finish — design, permits, install, inspection, utility approval — most projects are producing power in 4–8 weeks.
