Questions installers ask

Before you book.

Won't texting old leads annoy people?

Not the way we do it. The first touch is a soft, personal re-introduction that acknowledges time has passed and makes opting out effortless. These are people who asked your company for a quote — most stalled because life got busy, not because they said no. The ones who aren't interested tap out cleanly; the ones who are book a time.

Is contacting my old database even legal?

Yes — when it's done right. We only message first-party leads: people who gave your company their information and consent. Every number is registered with carriers under your business, quiet hours are enforced, any opt-out is honored instantly, and you own the full consent and message log. We never touch purchased or shared lists under your name.

My leads are one, two, even three years old. Are they too dead?

Old is normal — that's the point of the system. A homeowner who asked about solar in 2024 still owns the same roof and pays a bigger utility bill today. The list gets cleaned and segmented at onboarding, and the campaign is built for exactly this: polite, patient revival of people who stalled. This is also why we require 500+ leads — the math works on real databases.

What exactly counts as an "appointment"?

A verified homeowner with a $100+/month utility bill, stated 600+ credit, a viable roof, and a confirmed, reconfirmed sit on your calendar. No-shows don't count — they get replaced. Every booking comes with its full conversation transcript so you can see exactly how it was set.

What happens when a lead asks something the system can't answer?

It routes to a human — with the full conversation attached, so nothing gets asked twice. The system never bluffs pricing and never makes commitments you didn't script. You approve every script before anything sends.

How fast can this be live?

About one to two weeks from the strategy call. Most of that is carrier registration of your texting numbers — a compliance step we handle — plus list cleaning and your script sign-off. The guarantee clock starts when messages go live, and the first reactivated appointments typically book within the first week of sending.

What does it cost?

Founding-client pricing is quoted on the strategy call once we've seen your list size — and the math has to embarrass the price or we don't take the deal. For scale: it costs about a third of one human appointment setter, and the ten guaranteed sits alone are worth $1,500–$3,000 at market rates.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term lock-in. We earn it month to month — the 90-day guarantee exists so the risk sits with us, not you.
