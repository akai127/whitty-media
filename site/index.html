Questions installers ask

Before you book.

Will it sound robotic to my customers?

Hear it yourself — that's what the live demo is for. Modern voice AI holds natural conversations, handles interruptions, and answers real solar questions. Most homeowners never realize they weren't talking to your office.

What happens when the AI can't answer a question?

It transfers the call warmly to you or your team, with a text summary of who's calling and what they need. It never bluffs pricing or makes commitments you didn't script.

How fast can this be live?

Typically about a week from the strategy call: we script it around your offers, connect your calendar and CRM, test it with you, then switch it on.

Does it work with my CRM and calendar?

Yes — the system integrates with the major CRMs and booking calendars used in solar. If you're running spreadsheets, we'll set the pipeline up for you as part of the build.

What does it cost?

Every build is quoted on the strategy call based on your call volume and lead flow — but every plan costs less than a part-time receptionist, and unlike a receptionist it never sleeps, quits, or takes a sick day.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term lock-in to start. We earn the retainer month to month — the 90-day guarantee exists so the risk sits with us, not you.

Is the outreach compliant?

Yes. The system calls and texts your own opted-in leads — people who asked your company for a quote — with consent capture and opt-out built into every touchpoint. We don't do cold robocalls to purchased lists.
